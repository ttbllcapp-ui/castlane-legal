Sending feedback from inside the App (Settings → Report a problem) attaches what we already detected about your connection, which usually saves a round of questions.

My TV doesn't appear in the list

Four things account for almost every case. A VPN routes your traffic away from your own network, so nothing local can be found — turn it off and scan again. Your phone and television have to be on the same Wi-Fi network, and a guest network, or separate names for the 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands, count as different networks. Some routers have client isolation or AP isolation switched on, which stops devices seeing each other. And the television has to be awake, not in standby.

Netflix, Disney+ or Prime Video shows a black screen

Those services encrypt their video so it plays only inside their own apps. Every app of this kind hits the same wall; it is their restriction, not a fault in CastLane. Open the service's own app and use the AirPlay button inside it.

How do I stop casting?

Sending your screen to a television is handled by iOS, so it keeps going after you leave the App and no app is allowed to end it for you. Tap the AirPlay button on the now-playing bar and choose iPhone, or open Control Center and do the same there.

I can see my Chromecast but can't connect to it

This version connects to AirPlay devices — Apple TV and AirPlay 2 televisions. Chromecast devices are found and listed, and the App says plainly that they are not supported yet rather than letting you tap into a dead end. Support for them is planned.

The picture stutters or looks soft

Check the network reading on the home screen. It measures how quickly your television actually answers, which is a different thing from how many Wi-Fi bars your phone shows — a phone can have a strong signal and still a poor path to the television. Moving closer to the router, or using a 5GHz network, usually fixes it.

A video on a website can't be sent

Some sites, YouTube among them, assemble video inside the page rather than serving a file, so there is no address to hand a television. The App tells you when that has happened and offers screen mirroring, which works for anything.

Does CastLane collect my data?

No. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. See the privacy policy.
