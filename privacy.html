Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CastLane ("the App") runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours for your information to be sent to.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. The App has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking software of any kind. We do not know who you are, which devices you own, or what you cast.

LOCAL NETWORK ACCESS

iOS asks your permission for local network access so the App can list the televisions and streaming devices on your Wi-Fi. That search happens only on your own network, and the results, device names and how each one connects, stay on your phone. Nothing about your network is transmitted anywhere.

NETWORK MEASUREMENT

Before casting, the App measures how quickly the device you selected responds, so it can tell you what quality your network will actually support. This is a direct connection to that device on your own network. No measurement is reported to us.

PHOTOS AND VIDEOS

If you choose to cast a video from your library, iOS hands the App a copy of only the video you picked. It is played on your device and sent to your television. It is not uploaded, and it is removed when you are done.

THE BUILT-IN BROWSER

The App includes a browser for finding video on the web. Pages you open are loaded directly by your device, and the sites you visit see your device as they would in any browser, subject to their own privacy policies. We do not log, store, or transmit your browsing.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

A short list of what you recently cast, and your settings, are saved in local device storage so the App can offer them again. Deleting the App removes them.

WHEN YOU CONTACT US

If you send feedback from inside the App, it opens your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. If you leave the diagnostic section attached, it contains your connection type, whether a VPN is active, the measured speed, and app and iOS versions. It holds no personal information, and nothing about what you cast. You can delete it before sending.

THIRD PARTIES

The App contains no third-party software development kits, no advertising networks, and no analytics providers. Casting to Apple TV and AirPlay televisions is handled by Apple's own system features.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not directed at children under 13, and since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

CHANGES

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
